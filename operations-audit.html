What happens in the two hours

We sit down — in person in Pune, or on a call — as one business owner with another.

You describe how enquiries arrive, who handles them, and what happens next.

We trace one real enquiry from first message to closed sale. Where it slowed, who dropped it, how long it took.

We do the same for follow-ups, and for the work that keeps landing back on your desk.

We say what we would fix first, and what we would leave alone.

No slides. No pitch deck. It is a working session.

What the written report contains

The three bottlenecks, in your business, in plain language.

A number on each: hours lost per week, leads at risk per month, or rupees sitting in unchased follow-ups.

The sequence to fix them in — Time, then People, then Money — and why that order matters.

A clear recommendation: fix it yourself, fix it with us, or leave it alone.

You keep the report either way.
