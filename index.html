01

The Time Leak — you are the bottleneck

Delayed material. Missing workers. Angry customers. Follow-ups nobody made. You start your day firefighting and end it wondering where the day went. Your business runs only as fast as you can personally run.

→ AI answers, follows up, and chases — 24/7, without you.

You hire, train, and hope. They leave, underperform, or turn up late. The quality of your Tuesday depends on who showed up on Monday. Good people are rare — and the rare ones are expensive.

Missed leads. Forgotten follow-ups. Quotes sent two days late. This is the leak everyone chases first — and it's actually the result of the first two. Fix your time and your people problem, and the money leak closes on its own.
