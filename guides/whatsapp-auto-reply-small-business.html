WhatsApp auto reply for small business: the free setup, its limits, and what comes after

By Yash M., Founder — I run a manufacturing business in Pune. Our enquiry response went from "next morning" to under 60 seconds.

The short answer: the free WhatsApp Business app gives you three auto-reply tools (Away message, Greeting message, Quick replies) — set them up today, they take 10 minutes. But they only send fixed messages; they can't answer a single actual question. When enquiries become real conversations, you need an AI bot on the WhatsApp Business API.

Most guides stop at "download WhatsApp Business." That helps you for a week. This guide covers the full ladder — free tools, where they break, and what the next step actually costs — from an owner who climbed it.

Level 1 (free, today): the three built-in tools

  • Away message — Settings → Business tools → Away message. Set it for outside working hours. Write it like a human: "Thanks for messaging! We're at the factory till 7pm — you'll hear from us first thing tomorrow. For urgent orders, call [number]."
  • Greeting message — welcomes first-time customers automatically. One warm line + one question that moves things forward: "Welcome to [business]! Are you looking for a quote, an order update, or something else?"
  • Quick replies — saved answers triggered by a shortcut (/price, /address, /catalogue). Write your 6 most-sent messages once; your team stops typing them forever.

Do all three today. They cost nothing and already put you ahead of most competitors.

The free tools answer for you. They never answer the customer. Every real question still waits for a human — and in most Indian businesses, that human is the owner, at 9pm, after dinner.

Level 3: a real WhatsApp AI bot (the API)

An AI bot built on the official WhatsApp Business API can: answer FAQs from your actual catalogue, ask qualifying questions, capture name/requirement/city, book meetings into your calendar, escalate angry or unusual customers to you within minutes — in English, Hindi, or Hinglish, at 3pm or 3am.

Honest numbers: a fixed-scope bot like this is typically a ₹25,000–45,000 one-time build, live in about two weeks, with modest monthly running costs. In my own business this took enquiry response from "next morning, if someone remembered" to under 60 seconds, around the clock.

Which level are you?

  • Under ~10 enquiries a week → Level 1 is enough. Spend your time elsewhere.
  • Enquiries arrive after hours, or follow-ups slip → you're at Level 3's door. The leads you're losing at 9pm usually cost more per month than the bot costs once.

Frequently asked questions

Is the WhatsApp Business app really free?

Yes — the app and its three auto-reply tools cost nothing. Costs start only when you move to the Business API (needed for AI bots), which charges small per-conversation fees plus your builder's fee.

Will customers know they're talking to a bot?

With fixed auto-replies, instantly — and they don't love it. With a well-built AI bot, they mostly don't notice, because their question actually got answered. Customers hate waiting, not bots.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

That's exactly the right order. Set up Level 1 today; when the volume of unanswered evening enquiries starts hurting, that's your signal to build.
