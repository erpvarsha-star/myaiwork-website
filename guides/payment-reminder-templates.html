Payment reminder message templates that actually get you paid

Written by the owner — I run a manufacturing business in Pune and automated this exact problem in my own company first.

The short answer: a payment reminder that works is short, factual, and always contains three things — invoice number, exact amount, and your UPI details. Send four: 2 days before the due date, on the due date, 3 days after, and 7 days after. After the fourth, call. Below are the exact templates, in English and Hindi.

Chasing payments is the work every Indian business owner hates most — not because it's hard, but because it's awkward and endless. In my own business, reminders were the single biggest chunk of "work" that turned out to need no human at all. Here are the templates we use, free to copy.

The 4-message ladder (English)

Message 1 — 2 days before due date (friendly nudge) Hi [Name] ji, a gentle reminder — invoice #[No] for ₹[Amount] falls due on [Date]. UPI: [your@upi]. Thank you!
Message 2 — on the due date (polite, factual) Hi [Name] ji, invoice #[No] for ₹[Amount] is due today. Sharing our UPI for convenience: [your@upi]. Please share a screenshot once done — thank you!
Message 3 — 3 days overdue (firm, still warm) Hi [Name] ji, invoice #[No] for ₹[Amount] is now 3 days past due. If there's any issue with the invoice, please tell me today and I'll sort it. Otherwise requesting payment this week. UPI: [your@upi]
Message 4 — 7 days overdue (final message before the call) Hi [Name] ji, this is my fourth reminder for invoice #[No], ₹[Amount]. I'll call you tomorrow between [time]–[time] to close this. If payment is already made, please ignore and accept my thanks.

The same ladder in Hindi

संदेश 1 — देय तिथि से 2 दिन पहले नमस्ते [Name] जी, विनम्र अनुस्मारक — इनवॉइस #[No], ₹[Amount], देय तिथि [Date] है। UPI: [your@upi]। धन्यवाद!
संदेश 2 — देय तिथि पर नमस्ते [Name] जी, इनवॉइस #[No], ₹[Amount] आज देय है। UPI: [your@upi]। भुगतान के बाद स्क्रीनशॉट भेज दें — धन्यवाद!
संदेश 3 — 3 दिन विलंबित नमस्ते [Name] जी, इनवॉइस #[No] (₹[Amount]) की देय तिथि 3 दिन पहले निकल गई है। इनवॉइस में कोई समस्या हो तो आज बताएं, वरना इस सप्ताह भुगतान का अनुरोध है। UPI: [your@upi]
संदेश 4 — 7 दिन विलंबित (कॉल से पहले अंतिम) नमस्ते [Name] जी, इनवॉइस #[No], ₹[Amount] का यह चौथा अनुस्मारक है। कल [time]–[time] के बीच कॉल करूंगा। भुगतान हो गया हो तो कृपया अनदेखा करें — धन्यवाद।

Why this works (and what never to do)

  • Every message carries UPI details. Half of late payments are friction, not intent. Remove the friction.
  • Message 3 offers an out. "If there's an issue with the invoice" — real disputes surface here instead of festering for months.
  • Message 4 ends the messages. A fifth WhatsApp collects nothing. A phone call does.
  • Never threaten, never mention legal action in writing, never apologize for asking for your own money.

Frequently asked questions

How do I politely ask for payment on WhatsApp?

Short and factual: greeting, invoice number, amount, due date, UPI. No apologies, no paragraphs. Politeness is in the tone, not the length.

How many reminders should I send?

Four: 2 days before, on the due date, 3 days after, 7 days after. Then the owner calls. Message five collects nothing that message four didn't.

Can this be automated?

Yes — an automation reads your invoice list, sends each reminder on schedule, and stops on payment or dispute. It's typically a two-week build and usually the first leak we plug for clients.
